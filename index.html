Principal Investigators

Dinesh Manocha

Paul Chrisman Iribe Chair & Distinguished University Professor · Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering

Dinesh Manocha holds the Paul Chrisman Iribe Chair in Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland, and was previously a Distinguished Professor of Computer Science at UNC Chapel Hill. His work spans robotics, computer graphics, computational geometry and AI. He has published more than 850 papers, received 22 best paper and test-of-time awards, and supervised more than 63 PhD dissertations. Software from his group for collision detection, GPU computing and multi-agent simulation has been downloaded by over 500,000 users and licensed to more than 60 companies. He is a Fellow of ACM, AAAS, AAAI, IEEE and the National Academy of Inventors, a member of the ACM SIGGRAPH and IEEE VR Academies, and a co-founder of Impulsonic, which Valve acquired in 2016.

Ming C. Lin

Distinguished University Professor · Dr. Barry Mersky and Capital One E-Nnovate Endowed Professor

Ming C. Lin is a Distinguished University Professor at the University of Maryland, where she previously served as the Elizabeth Stevinson Iribe Chair of Computer Science. She is also a Distinguished Professor Emerita at UNC Chapel Hill and an Amazon Scholar with Amazon Robotics, and earned her B.S., M.S. and Ph.D. at UC Berkeley. Her research covers AI and machine learning, computational robotics, differentiable physics, physically-based simulation, virtual reality, sound rendering, haptics and geometric computing, with more than 400 refereed publications and four books. She is a Fellow of the National Academy of Inventors, ACM, IEEE and Eurographics, a member of the SIGGRAPH and IEEE VR Academies, a former Editor-in-Chief of IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, and a co-founder of Impulsonic, which Valve acquired in 2016.
